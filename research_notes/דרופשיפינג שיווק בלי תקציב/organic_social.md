Method note: WebSearch returned summarised results. WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy for tiktok.com, shopify.com, minea.com and support.google.com, so I could not read primary policy pages directly. Most findings below come from secondary sources (blogs, SaaS vendors, SEO agencies). Many of those sell tools or courses, so treat them as weak evidence. ## 1. Which organic channel has the best ROI for a beginner in 2026? Platform changes and country availability

### Takeaway
No source gives a controlled ROI comparison across channels. The evidence points to short-form video (TikTok first, with Reels and Shorts as free cross-posts) as the fastest way to get discovery with no followers, and to Pinterest and Google free listings as slower, compounding, search-driven channels. For an Israel-based seller, TikTok Shop is not available. Organic TikTok therefore means driving traffic to your own store via the bio link.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok US operations moved to TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC on Jan 22, 2026. Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold 15% each, and ByteDance holds 19.9%. The recommendation algorithm is to be retrained on US user data and hosted in Oracle's US cloud. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/16/tiktok-us-joint-venture-user-data-no-mass-exodus-oracle-mgx-silver-lake-larry-ellison-trump.html); [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/01/the-tiktok-us-saga-is-done-with-new-joint-venture-deal.html)
- The retraining affects US users. I found no hard evidence that organic reach for small accounts has changed materially. The same source says content weighting could technically be adjusted but there is no hard evidence it has happened. — [TikTok Substack analysis, "Algorithm Under Control"](https://tiktoktiktoktiktok.substack.com/p/algorithm-under-control). Single secondary source.
- The new TikTok US terms of use add collection of precise location and data on interactions with AI tools. — same CNBC and secondary coverage above.
- TikTok Shop is reported live in 21 markets as of Aug 2026: US, Mexico, Brazil, UK, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Austria, Belgium, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Singapore, Japan. Israel is NOT on the list. — [SocialTale](https://socialtale.co/blog/tiktok-shop-countries-live-2026); [TikTok Newsroom, Europe expansion](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-shop-expands-across-europe?lang=en-150). The list comes from a third-party blog plus a search-summary and is not confirmed on TikTok's own seller page [UNVERIFIED-PRIMARY]. A Shopify Israel page on TikTok Shop exists ([Shopify IL](https://www.shopify.com/il/blog/tiktok-shopping)) but I could not read it.
- Instagram: Reels are ranked mainly on watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach. Sends reportedly carry about 3-5x the weight of likes for reaching non-followers. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers) (secondary, attributes the claim to Mosseri; not verified).
- Instagram originality enforcement: reposted Reels are replaced by the original in recommendations. Accounts publishing unoriginal content 10 or more times in 30 days reportedly stop being recommended. Enforcement widened to carousels in April 2026. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm). Matters for anyone reposting TikTok videos with the watermark or lifting supplier footage.
- Instagram Reels now run up to 20 minutes, but short still wins for discovery. Mosseri's Dec 31, 2025 memo reportedly said "the polished, perfect aesthetic is dead" and that Instagram will favour raw, human content over AI-generated content. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers) (secondary).
- YouTube Shorts: about 200+ billion daily views (June 2025 figure). — [Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics) (secondary; STALE by about 16 months).
- YouTube Shopping affiliate eligibility was reportedly tied to YouTube Partner Program membership from Mar 27, 2026. The expanded tier needs 500 subscribers plus 3,000 long-form watch hours or 3M Shorts views. This is for tagging partner retailers' products, not your own dropship store. — [VirVid](https://virvid.ai/blog/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-shorts-guide-2026) (low-quality secondary).
- Pinterest: initial traffic reportedly appears within 2-4 weeks. Meaningful traffic comes at 3-6 months and significant revenue at 6-12 months. Half of site visits reportedly occur about 3.5 months after pinning and half of orders about 2.5 months after. — [84pins](https://84pins.com/how-long-does-pinterest-marketing-take/); [Pinterest/TechCrunch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/?p=914231); [LaunchMyStore](https://launchmystore.io/blog/pinterest-marketing-ecommerce-traffic-sales). Vendor and agency claims, not controlled data.

### Inferences
- Best ROI for a beginner with no budget and no audience is probably TikTok plus cross-posting the same original video to Reels and Shorts, because discovery does not depend on followers. This is my inference. No source ranks channels with data.
- For an Israeli seller the realistic targets are English-speaking or EU/UK/US audiences. A Hebrew-only store has a small market, and TikTok Shop checkout is unavailable regardless.
- Pinterest and Google free listings are the "set and compound" layer. Do them in parallel at low effort but do not expect sales in the first 4-8 weeks.
- Reddit and Facebook groups are low-volume and high-ban-risk for pure promotion (see section 6). They are better used for research than for traffic.

### Gaps
- No credible head-to-head data on organic reach or sales per hour across TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Pinterest, Reddit and Facebook groups.
- Could not confirm from TikTok's own page that Israel is excluded. Also could not confirm whether the TikTok-Shopify channel app works for Israel-based merchants.
- No evidence found on whether the US joint-venture algorithm retrain changed reach for new small accounts, or on Pinterest Shopping changes beyond merchant guideline pages.

## 2. Platform policies on dropshipping and organic promotion; ban risks

### Takeaway
No major platform bans dropshipping as such. All of them penalise the behaviours that typically go with low-effort dropshipping: misleading shipping and delivery claims, stolen content, unoriginal reposts, unlabeled realistic AI, spam and low-quality products. Account bans on a new account are cheap for the platform to issue and hard to appeal.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok: personal accounts need 1,000+ followers for a clickable website link. Business accounts can add a link with no follower minimum, but lose access to the full commercial music library. One link only. — [Stan](https://stan.store/blog/tiktok-link-bio-requirements-2026-guide/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/how-to-add-link-tiktok-bio-complete-guide). Third-party blogs, not TikTok's own page [UNVERIFIED-PRIMARY].
- TikTok has reportedly tightened dropshipping rules in ads and shop: disclose product origin and realistic delivery time, no before/after images in ads, and poor product quality or complaints can lead to listing removal or suspension. — [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-dropshipping-ecommerce-ad-compliance-2026); [EcomBiz.ai](https://ecombiz.ai/articles/what-you-can-and-cant-do-on-tiktok-shop-as-a-dropshipper/). These mostly concern ads and TikTok Shop, not purely organic posts [UNVERIFIED-PRIMARY].
- TikTok AI labeling: creators must label AI-generated or significantly edited content showing realistic people or scenes. The label is a disclosure, not a ban. Unlabeled content may be removed, restricted or labeled by TikTok, and C2PA Content Credentials are used for automatic detection. — [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/tiktoks-2026-ai-labeling-rules-and-what-they-signal-for-platform-governance/); [Cinerads](https://www.cinerads.com/blog/tiktok-ai-content-policy); [Creators Agency](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/youtube-tiktok-ai-disclosure-rules-2026)
- Meta: nothing in Meta's Commerce Policies explicitly prohibits or permits dropshipping. Policies target bad customer experience and transparency. — [Grokipedia summary of Meta Commerce Policies](https://grokipedia.com/page/Facebook_Commerce_Policies); [Agrowth](https://agrowth.io/blogs/facebook-ads/facebook-dropshipping-ban). Weak sources, mostly about ads.
- Instagram: accounts publishing unoriginal content 10+ times in 30 days lose recommendations. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- YouTube: from July 15, 2025 the "repetitious content" policy became "inauthentic content" (mass-produced or templated videos with minimal variation are not monetizable). AI use does not itself disqualify. Originality does. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/youtube-updates-repetitive-content-policy/). This concerns monetization, not whether you can promote your own store.
- Pinterest Merchant Guidelines: merchants must not be affiliate marketers or wholesale retailers. They need a high-quality site and product pages and a clear, easy-to-find return policy with contact details. Merchants are responsible for orders, delivery and disputes. — [Pinterest Merchant guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/merchant-guidelines). Primary source.
- Pinterest Verified Merchant Program reportedly gates shopping-surface eligibility. — [Tailwind](https://www.tailwindapp.com/blog/pinterest-verified-merchant-program); [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/pinterest-product-tagging-catalog-ads-compliance-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Safest posture: show real or original footage you made, state shipping origin and times honestly on the site and in videos, label realistic AI avatars or voices, never use fake scarcity or fake reviews, and keep a visible returns policy.
- Using AliExpress or supplier clips with logos removed is the pattern most exposed to originality and unoriginal-content rules. Order a sample and film it yourself.
- Use a dedicated business account only if you need the early link. Otherwise reach 1,000 followers on a creator account, or use a link-in-bio tool or pinned comment workaround (see sources above).

### Gaps
- I could not read TikTok's Community Guidelines, TikTok Shop Seller policy, Meta Commerce Policies or YouTube spam policy directly. Verify before publication.
- No data on actual ban rates for dropshipping-promoting new accounts. Only anecdotes and agency warnings.

## 3. Content formats, hooks, cadence, videos needed before first sale; real case studies vs guru claims

### Takeaway
Disclosed numbers are mostly guru or tool-vendor content with survivorship bias. The honest evidence is that organic TikTok dropshipping is high-variance. Some people post 75-150+ videos before anything gets traction, and the viral winners are the exceptions that case-study blogs select.

### Cited Findings
- Practitioner-style example (Shopify Community, forum post, unverified): about 75 videos in 30 days, most under 1,000 views and plateauing within about 4 hours. — [Shopify Community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/rate-my-tiktok-marketting-videos/391296)
- One reported success posted 100+ videos before finding a "reaction video" format, then about 50 more reaction videos before the big viral hit. — same Shopify Community search result, [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/rate-my-tiktok-marketting-videos/391296) (anecdote, unverified).
- Case-study claims to treat as guru or vendor content: "$11,000 in 7 days" ([AliDropship](https://alidropship.com/11k-in-7-days-tiktok-dropshipping/)), "$60K in 30 days" ([AliDropship](https://alidropship.com/tiktok-organic-dropshipping/)), "$5,381" in five days from one semi-viral 200k-view video ([Tabcut](https://www.tabcut.com/blog/post/i-tried-tiktok-organic-dropshipping-and-made-5381)), "$13K in 25 days" ([Sellvia](https://sellvia.com/blog/organic-tiktok-dropshipping-10k-month/)). AliDropship sells dropshipping software, Tabcut sells a product-research tool and Sellvia sells a dropshipping service. None disclose a verifiable number of videos before first sale. Do not treat as typical.
- Forum evidence of failure: many Shopify Community threads of owners with traffic but zero sales, citing site design, product, pricing and trust issues rather than content alone. — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/zero-sales/354665); [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/lots-of-sessions-but-no-sales/370230)
- Instagram favours content that holds attention and gets shared, with DM sends a key signal. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- Viewers reportedly scroll away within 2-5 seconds, so the hook must come first. — Shopify Community search summary, [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/rate-my-tiktok-marketting-videos/391296)

### Inferences
- Formats commonly recommended in these sources and consistent with the signals above: problem-solution, hands-on demo, POV/UGC-style, reaction and "satisfying" product use. This is a general pattern, not tested by me.
- A reasonable plan is 1-3 videos a day for 30-60 days across one niche or product, then double down on what gets above-average watch time. This is my inference from the 75-150 video anecdotes, not a sourced benchmark.
- The same short original video can be uploaded natively to TikTok, Reels and Shorts (without the TikTok watermark), which costs minimal extra effort.

### Gaps
- No credible, independently verified dataset on videos posted before first sale. I found only anecdotes.
- Could not verify the case-study numbers. Product, country and audience details are not disclosed.
- I did not find r/dropship, r/shopify or r/TikTokShop threads directly (search returned Shopify Community only). A further pass on those subreddits would help.

## 4. Free AI and content tools (2026 limits) and AI-disclosure issues

### Takeaway
You can make acceptable faceless or demo videos for $0 with CapCut free, Canva free, an LLM for scripts and free TTS, but AI video generation is mostly paid or heavily capped. Realistic AI visuals and voices need a label on TikTok, and AI-generated "people" using the product are the biggest policy and trust risk.

### Cited Findings
- CapCut free: export up to 1080p (4K is Pro). No watermark on standard exports and free templates, but AI-template exports and some premium-effect outputs carry a non-commercial watermark. The free plan includes auto-captions (up to about ten minutes per video), text-to-speech, and background removal on limited monthly credits. — [Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/capcut-pro-vs-free/); [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/video-editing/capcut/free-plan/); [Zsky](https://zsky.ai/blog/is-capcut-ai-free). Secondary, limits change often.
- Canva free: as of June 2026 only 5 lifetime AI video credits, clips max 4 seconds, Veo 3-powered generator reportedly not on the free plan. — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/canva-ai-video-generator-review); [Morphed](https://morphed.app/blog/canva-ai-video-generator-pricing)
- ElevenLabs free: 10,000 credits per month (about 10 minutes of TTS). Free-plan audio requires attribution and excludes commercial rights, so it is not suitable for store promo videos. — [Smallest.ai](https://smallest.ai/blog/elevenlabs-pricing-plans-cost-what-you-get-in-2026); [Anygen](https://www.anygen.io/showcase/elevenlabs-free/index.html)
- Hebrew TTS free options exist (Maestra, FreeTTS, Kapwing, Fliki with 4 Hebrew voices). Commercial-use terms vary and were not checked. — [Fliki](https://fliki.ai/voices/hebrew); [Maestra](https://maestra.ai/tools/text-to-speech/hebrew); [FreeTTS](https://freetts.com/text-to-speech/HebrewTTS)
- Gemini: from Oct 9, 2026 users without a Google AI subscription are limited to Flash-Lite. The free tier has a 32K context window. — [Shattered.io](https://shattered.io/google-gemini-free-flash-lite-oct-9-2026/); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/just-days-after-gemini-argon-launch-google-updates-ai-plans-to-limit-free-use-significantly/). Check the current Google announcement [UNVERIFIED-PRIMARY]. Veo is bundled into paid Google plans. — [Eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sora-2-pricing)
- Meta Vibes video generation is free inside the Meta AI app and meta.ai during rollout, with credit and rate limits. Meta has announced premium tiers. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/meta-vibes/); [FelloAI](https://felloai.com/meta-ai-video-generator/). Availability by country, including Israel, was not confirmed.
- Sora: one source reports the Sora web and mobile apps were discontinued in April 2026 and that Sora was never free. — [Zsky](https://zsky.ai/blog/is-sora-free). Single low-quality source, verify before stating as fact.
- TikTok requires an AIGC label for realistic AI content and can auto-detect via C2PA. Burned-in disclaimers are accepted in ads. — [Cinerads](https://www.cinerads.com/blog/tiktok-ai-content-policy); [UGCVids](https://ugcvids.ai/blog/tiktok-ai-content-disclosure-rules-2026)
- Instagram reportedly de-prioritises AI-generated content in favour of raw, human content in 2026. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)

### Inferences
- Zero-cost stack: Claude or ChatGPT free for scripts, hooks and Hebrew-to-English translation. A phone for filming a sample product. CapCut free for editing and captions. Canva free for thumbnails and Pinterest pins. A free TTS only if its terms allow commercial use.
- Avoid fake AI "customers" or AI-generated testimonials. They carry both label and consumer-protection risk (the latter is my inference).
- Tool limits above are volatile and mostly from SEO blogs. The report should say "check current limits" rather than quote exact numbers as stable.

### Gaps
- No primary-source confirmation of CapCut, Canva, ElevenLabs, Gemini or Meta Vibes limits.
- Did not research Meta's AI-label policy for Instagram or YouTube's altered/synthetic content disclosure in detail.
- Did not confirm Hebrew voice quality or licence terms.

## 5. SEO, Pinterest evergreen traffic and Google free listings

### Takeaway
Google Merchant Center free listings cost nothing and need no ads account. They are available in Israel per the sources found, but require a verified domain, return and shipping policies, price match and business verification. Pinterest is slow to start but compounds.

### Cited Findings
- Free listings need a verified and claimed domain, accurate business info, shipping and return info, and policy compliance. Merchant Center Next also checks legal business name, address, phone, domain ownership and business model (retailer vs dropshipper vs manufacturer). — [FeedOps](https://feedops.com/feedops/google-shopping-free-listings/); [Advisable](https://www.advisable.com/insights/how-to-list-products-on-google-for-free-2026)
- Minimum attributes: id, title, price, description, availability, link, image_link. GTIN is required where it exists, otherwise MPN. — [FeedOps](https://feedops.com/feedops/google-shopping-free-listings/)
- Price mismatch between feed and landing page is the most common disqualifier. — FeedOps, same link.
- No Google Ads account is needed for free listings. They are on by default in Merchant Center Next. — [DataFeedWatch](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/free-product-listings)
- Israel appears in Google's lists of supported countries for Shopping ads and free listings, with ILS supported. Free local listings are also offered there. — [Google Merchant Center Help: supported languages and currencies](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160637?hl=en); [Google: free listings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13889434?hl=en) (surfaced via search, page not fetched [UNVERIFIED-PRIMARY])
- The Shopify Google & YouTube app is available in Israel in English and Hebrew, and synced products can appear in Google Shopping tab listings for free. — [Shopify IL Google channel](https://www.shopify.com/il/blog/shopify-google-channel); [Google: Shopify app](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16267554?hl=en)
- Pinterest: Product Pins carry price and availability. Rich Pins and merchant guidelines apply. A Pinterest pin can drive clicks for 6-12 months or longer. — [Pinterest rich pins](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/rich-pins); [Pinterest merchant guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/merchant-guidelines); [Kayla Watkins](https://kaylawatkins.com/how-long-do-pinterest-results-last/)

### Inferences
- Free listings are the most "buyer-intent" free channel, but a new, thin dropship store can be held back by missing policy pages, price mismatches, no reviews and misrepresentation checks. Expect delays or disapprovals. Not quantified in any source I found.
- Pinterest works best for visual categories (home, decor, gifts, beauty, fashion, DIY). Make several pin images per product with keyword-rich titles, boards and descriptions.

### Gaps
- Time to approval and typical disapproval rates for new dropshipping stores in Merchant Center: not found.
- Google "Misrepresentation" policy treatment of dropshippers was not read. Pinterest Product Pin rules for dropshippers were not read in full.
- Pinterest availability and Shopping features for Israel-based merchants not confirmed.

## 6. Community tactics: Reddit and Facebook groups

### Takeaway
Pure promotion gets removed or banned. Reddit enforcement is mostly per subreddit and 61% of surveyed subreddits ban self-promotion or allow it only under about 9:1. Facebook admins set their own rules and Facebook can also act on spam sitewide.

### Cited Findings
- Reddit's 9:1 rule was demoted in May 2017 from a sitewide policy to a guideline. Individual subreddits often enforce their own version. In a study of 49 founder-pitch subreddits, 61% banned self-promotion or allowed only 9:1 (39% ban outright, 22% allow with 9:1). — [OneUp study](https://oneup.today/blogs/reddit-selfpromo-rules-study-2026); [RedReplier](https://redreplier.com/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rule). The OneUp study sampled SaaS and founder subreddits, so it does not necessarily match shopping or niche-hobby subreddits.
- Brand-new accounts that lead with product links are flagged fastest. Shadowbans (posts invisible to others without notice) hit accounts doing rapid self-promotion or multi-subreddit links. Test by viewing your profile logged out. — [Redship](https://redship.io/learn/how-to-avoid-getting-banned-marketing-reddit); [Reddiscout](https://reddiscout.com/blog/reddit-marketing-without-getting-banned/)
- Facebook: group admins set rules. Some groups ban selling, some allow it on set days, some require active membership. Facebook's Community Standards also apply regardless of the group's rules, and identical posts across many groups raise ban risk. — [MultipleGroupPoster](https://multiplegroupposter.com/blog/facebook-group-promotion-rules/); [GroupPosting](https://groupposting.com/group-posting/mastering-facebook-group-rules-the-2026-guide-to-growth-and-compliance/). Both sources sell group-posting tools.
- A Medium piece reports that Facebook changed group rules without announcement. — [Medium](https://medium.com/seriously-youre-making-my-head-hurt/facebook-has-changed-the-rules-for-groups-and-nobody-was-told-495db62def6e) (not read, low reliability).

### Inferences
- What works instead: build a real account first, answer questions in niche communities, share genuinely useful posts, put the link in your profile rather than posts where the rules allow it, and use designated promo threads. Use Hebrew-language Israeli Facebook groups for local audiences. These are standard practice and are not backed by conversion data in what I found.
- Treat these communities as a source of customer language (questions, complaints, product ideas) to feed video scripts.

### Gaps
- No data on sales actually generated from Reddit or Facebook groups for dropshippers.
- Did not verify Reddit's current ad-hoc policy on self-promotion from the primary Reddit help page, or any Israeli Facebook group rules.

## 7. Realistic expectations: time to first sale, organic conversion rate, hours per week

### Takeaway
Plan for weeks to months and for conversion near 1%. Organic TikTok traffic converts at roughly 0.5-1.0% per one benchmark, and social traffic generally at 1-2%. Hours per week and time to first sale have no reliable sourced benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- Organic TikTok traffic conversion benchmark of 0.5-1.0% (diluted by viewers who cannot buy or are outside shipping countries). — [Ringly / TikTok Shop stats](https://www.ringly.io/blog/tiktok-shop-statistics-2026); [TikAdSuite](https://tikadsuite.com/blog/tiktok-conversion-rate-benchmarks/). Vendor benchmarks, not audited data.
- Another set of benchmarks: organic social 1.2-1.8%, TikTok 0.8-1.4%. Median Shopify store 1.4%, average 1.8%, top 20% at 3.2%+, top 10% at 4.5%+. — [Eevy](https://eevy.ai/blog/shopify-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026); [Uptek](https://uptek.com/shopify-statistics/conversion-rate/). Secondary aggregators. The two benchmark sets disagree somewhat, so give a range.
- Pinterest: first traffic in 2-4 weeks, meaningful traffic in 3-6 months. — [84pins](https://84pins.com/how-long-does-pinterest-marketing-take/)
- Anecdotal range for TikTok: about 75 videos in 30 days with no traction, versus 150+ videos before a viral hit. — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/rate-my-tiktok-marketting-videos/391296) (anecdote).

### Inferences
- Arithmetic example, my own: at about 1% conversion, 100 sales needs about 10,000 store visits, and one sale needs about 100 visits. A video with 5,000 views and a 2-3% click-through gives 100-150 visits, which implies roughly one sale per video that performs that well. Most videos will not.
- A beginner doing 1-3 short videos a day plus store setup probably needs 10-20 hours per week for the first 2 months. This is my estimate and is not sourced.
- A first sale from organic traffic within 2-8 weeks is plausible only with consistent posting and a trustworthy site. Many will see no sales for 2-3 months. This is not backed by a dataset.
- Margins matter. Low-price items with low organic conversion and long shipping from China tend to produce refunds and chargebacks (inference, supported only by policy warnings about delivery times).

### Gaps
- No credible sourced data on hours per week or median time to first organic sale for beginner dropshippers.
- Conversion rates for Pinterest, Reels, Shorts, Reddit and Facebook groups traffic specifically were not found.
- Israel-specific data (payments, VAT/customs, Hebrew-store demand, Israeli social usage) was not researched here and should come from other researchers.
