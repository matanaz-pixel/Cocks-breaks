# Niche/Product Selection and Zero-Budget Demand Validation for Beginner Dropshippers (as of Oct 2026)

> Research caveats (read first): (1) WebFetch was blocked by the network egress proxy for almost every domain (trueprofit, printful, spocket, adlibrary, ads.tiktok.com, etc.), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result summaries, NOT from reading full pages or primary sources. Treat figures as "reported by", and verify before relying on them. (2) Almost all dropshipping-statistics sources are vendors, app makers, affiliate-review sites, or course sellers with an incentive to be bullish (or to sell a tool). I flag this per item. (3) I found NO primary/independent academic or Shopify-published base rate for dropshipping success. (4) Pricing/free-tier details change often; third-party pricing pages are cited, official pages were not readable. Anything with a date before 2025 is flagged "potentially stale".

## 1. Criteria that actually predict success (margin, price band, AOV, shipping, problem-solving vs impulse, repeat purchase, returns, IP risk)

### Takeaway
Consensus across (mostly vendor) sources: net margin is thin (beginners under 10%, experienced 10-20%), so you need a high gross margin (reported 45-50%+ for low-ticket) and a price band of roughly $30-$100 where the dollar profit per order can absorb ad cost, fees, and returns. Problem-solving, lightweight, non-branded, low-return products are favoured; post-2025 tariff/customs changes (US de minimis ended, EU EUR 3 duty from July 2026) make China-direct shipping to US/EU materially costlier and are a new first-order selection criterion.

### Cited Findings
- Net margins: typical Shopify dropshipping net profit 10-20% after all expenses; experienced sellers 15-20%, beginners often below 10%. Vendor/affiliate content, no primary data. — [Branvas via search summary](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/is-dropshipping-profitable); [TrueProfit](https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-profit-margin); [Dropcommerce](https://blog.dropcommerce.com/posts/dropshipping-profit-margins)
- Gross margin needed: "at least 45-50% gross margin to absorb ad costs, platform fees, and returns" at scale; a 60% gross margin can shrink to about 24% net after ads, fees, returns. Another source says typical dropshipping gross margins are only 10-30% (conflicting numbers; definitions of "gross margin" differ, some include only product cost, some include shipping). — [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/is-dropshipping-profitable); [TrueProfit](https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-profit-margin)
- Price band / AOV: one source (ProductLair-type data, vendor; unverified methodology) reports $30-$50 products at about 71% product margin and about $34 profit per order; $50-$100 at about 67% margin and about $51 profit per order; a $50-100 product at 60% margin yields about 4.4x the profit per order of a sub-$10 product at 75% margin. Rule of thumb in the same results: low-ticket needs 50%+ margin, high-ticket can work at 15-25%. — [ProductLair](https://productlair.com/blog/dropshipping-profit-margins-by-category) and related results in the [search listing](https://alidropship.com/best-problem-solving-dropshipping-products/) (figures from search summary only; treat as unverified)
- Conversion rate: Shopify store median about 1.4%-1.8% (2024-2025); 2.5% is above average; top 20% reach 3.2%+, top 10% 4.7%+. By category: beauty about 2.6% (one source) to 4.9% (another), home and furniture 0.8-1.6%, jewelry/luxury about 0.6%. Sources disagree on category numbers. These are all-store benchmarks, a brand-new zero-trust store with cold traffic will usually sit below the median (inference). — [Littledata](https://www.littledata.io/ecommerce-conversion-rate); [Growthsuite](https://www.growthsuite.net/resources/shopify-conversion-rate/complete-guide/benchmarks-by-industry); [Enavi](https://www.enavi.co/blogs/shopify-conversion-rate-benchmarks)
- Return rates: overall US ecommerce about 19.3% of online sales returned in 2025 (cited to NRF 2025 Retail Returns Landscape); apparel 20-40%, home/furniture 15-23%, electronics 8-15% (consumer electronics about 10-11% online), pet products under about 8-10%. — [Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com/learn/ecommerce-return-rates); [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-return-rate); [Branvas return rates](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/ecommerce-return-rate-by-category)
- Ad-cost inflation: Meta CPMs reportedly up 89% since 2020; average ecommerce CAC up 60% over five years; dropshipping CPC up 63% since 2020 (attributed to "Shopify 2024 e-commerce report", not verified; potentially stale/2024 data). — [Search summary of Branvas/TrueProfit/Alibaba results](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/is-dropshipping-profitable)
- Problem-solving vs impulse: multiple practitioner/vendor sources claim problem-solving products (health discomfort, household inefficiency, pet problems) get higher CTR, lower cost per purchase, and justify higher prices than lifestyle/impulse items. Opinion, not controlled data. — [Alidropship](https://alidropship.com/best-problem-solving-dropshipping-products/)
- Repeat purchase / giftability: recommended in niche-guides (men's grooming, fragrance/cosmetics, smart-home add-ons) because repeat buyers lower blended CAC; this is opinion from content sites. — [SideHustleSchool via search summary](https://sidehustleschool.com/guides/is-dropshipping-worth-it/); [Dropbuild](https://www.dropbuild.com/blog/is-dropshipping-oversaturated)
- IP/trademark risk: Shopify can suspend the whole store, freeze payouts, and terminate all stores owned by the same person on a trademark complaint; no "three strikes" guarantee; knockoffs sourced from AliExpress/Alibaba are described as the #1 source of trademark violations. Branded goods, anime/character merch, and replicas are repeatedly listed as "avoid". — [Shop-Shield Shopify trademark guide 2026](https://shop-shield.com/blog/shopify-trademark-takedown-guide); [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-to-avoid-copyright-infringement-claims-as-a-new-dropshipper/418128); [ScaleOrder niches to avoid](https://scaleorder.com/blogs/i-tested-6-winning-niches-heres-why-they-failed-dropshipping-niches-to-avoid-in-2026/)
- Shipping weight/fragility: bulky, fragile, or complex-logistics goods are on "avoid" lists (shipping eats margin, higher damage/return rates). — [ScaleOrder](https://scaleorder.com/blogs/i-tested-6-winning-niches-heres-why-they-failed-dropshipping-niches-to-avoid-in-2026/)
- US tariff/customs: US ended the $800 de minimis exemption on Aug 29, 2025 (China/HK lost it on May 2, 2025); duties now apply to every package, reported 5-25%+ by category. — [TaxCloud](https://taxcloud.com/sales-tax-radar/us-de-minimis-exemption-ends-2025/); [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/blog/de-minimis-exemption-suspension); [Syncee](https://syncee.com/blog/drop-shipping/de-minimis-exemption-is-over/)
- US tariff specifics for 2026 are inconsistent across sources: one result says China tariffs were cut from 145% to 30% and an extra 12.5% Section 301 tariff was added on July 24, 2026; another says China-origin small parcels faced 54% ad valorem or a $100 flat fee. I could not verify these numbers; treat as UNVERIFIED and check current USTR/CBP guidance. — [OneShipPros](https://oneshippros.com/china-tariffs-2026-how-they-affect-your-dropshipping-business/); [TrueProfit tariffs](https://trueprofit.io/blog/tariffs-effect-on-dropshipping)
- EU: EUR 3 customs duty per HS-code line on low-value (up to EUR 150) parcels from July 1, 2026, interim until the Customs Data Hub (about mid-2028); a separate handling fee of about EUR 2 per parcel expected from November 2026 (reported as "expected"). IOSS registration relevant for VAT. — [Logistics Plus](https://www.logisticsplus.com/eu-de-minimis-in-effect-on-july-1-2026/); [Global VAT Compliance](https://www.globalvatcompliance.com/globalvatnews/eu-low-value-customs-duty/); [Baker Tilly SEE](https://bakertillysee.com/insights/major-eu-customs-update-e3-duty-on-low-value-e-commerce-imports-starting-july-2026/)
- Shipping times: AliExpress Standard to the US reported 15-30 business days; local-warehouse items 7-15 days; premium shipping 5-10 days but $15-$40 per package. — [USAdrop](https://usadrop.com/aliexpress-standard-shipping/); [DSers](https://www.dsers.com/blog/aliexpress-fast-shipping/) (via search summary)
- Israel-specific: Shopify Payments is not available to Israeli businesses; Israeli stores must use third-party gateways (Stripe availability for Israel, PayPal, local clearing providers) and pay an extra Shopify third-party transaction fee. — [Shopify Israel blog](https://www.shopify.com/il/blog/accept-payments-online); [Preneuriat](https://preneuriat.com/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-israel); [CartDNA](https://www.cartdna.com/shopify-payment-guide/middle-east/israel)
- Budget-relevant platform cost: Shopify's current promo reported as 3 days free then $1/month for 3 months on Basic/Grow/Advanced, monthly billing only, card needed for the $1 stage; regular Basic about $39/mo. Not literally zero cost. — [DoDropshipping](https://dodropshipping.com/shopify-free-trial/); [PageFly](https://pagefly.io/blogs/shopify/shopify-1-dollar)

### Inferences
- A practical screening checklist for a zero-budget beginner: landed cost under about 30-40% of selling price; selling price about $30-$100; light and non-fragile; no brand/character/trademark; not apparel; ships from a local/EU/US warehouse or has tolerable duty exposure for the target market; has a clear "problem" angle with visual demonstration.
- With a 1.4-2% conversion and an ad-based model, break-even ROAS math requires margin well above the 30% commonly quoted; with zero ad budget the gating factor is organic reach, which makes content-friendly (demonstrable) products the main criterion.
- For an Israel-based seller, selling to the US or EU while sourcing from China is hit by new duties on the customer side and by the lack of Shopify Payments on the payment side; consider choosing target markets and platform (see gaps) accordingly.

### Gaps
- No primary source (Shopify, NRF original report, Meta) was read directly; all numbers are via secondary summaries.
- No independent data on the share of beginner dropshippers whose gross margin exceeds 45-50%.
- Could not verify the current US tariff rate on China-origin parcels in Oct 2026 (sources conflict).
- Israeli-specific import/tax/VAT rules for the seller (and Israeli customs for any Israeli buyers) were not researched.

## 2. Free research tools: what each free tier actually allows in 2026

### Takeaway
Genuinely free and useful: Google Trends, TikTok Creative Center (including Top Products), Pinterest Trends, Meta Ad Library (active ads only, weak signals), Amazon Best Sellers pages, Reddit, CJ Dropshipping (free account, no monthly fee). Paid spy tools mostly offer only trials or tiny credit allowances; Minea has a perpetual 200-credit free plan, PiPiAds a 500-credit trial, Dropship.io no permanent free plan.

### Cited Findings
- Google Trends: free; shows relative interest 0-100 (100 = peak in the chosen window/region), not absolute volume, so a low-volume term can look identical to a blockbuster; it is subsampled and has resolution problems for low values. Useful for trend direction/seasonality and comparing terms, not market size. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-trends/); [Glimpse limitations](https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/advantages-disadvantages/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-trends/)
- TikTok Creative Center: free, available to guests without an account; includes Top Ads, Trend Discovery (hashtags, songs), Keyword Insights, Top Products (products advertised on TikTok linked to the ads promoting them), plus creative tools. Exact result limits/regions per view were not verifiable (official page blocked). — [TikTok official page (listed in results)](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/tiktok-creative-center?lang=en); [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-center-guide); [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/tiktok-creative-center)
- Meta Ad Library: free; search by keyword, filter active ads/country. For commercial ads it shows only currently active ads (no spend, no impressions, no conversions); political/issue ads are kept 7 years; ads delivered in EU/UK are archived 1 year; EU commercial ads give a reach figure but no spend. The common heuristic "ads running longest are profitable" is an inference, not a platform-provided signal (the library shows "started running on" dates, but no run-length filter or spend). — [AdLibrary: what Meta Ad Library doesn't show](https://adlibrary.com/posts/what-meta-ad-library-doesnt-show-you-2026) (vendor competitor, may exaggerate gaps); [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ads-library-api); [Zik Analytics how-to](https://www.zikanalytics.com/academy/shopify/tips-and-tricks-to-increase-sales-shopify/facebook-ad-library-how-to-use-meta-ad-library-to-find-winning-ads)
- Pinterest Trends: free (public view; fuller filters/demographics with a free Pinterest business account); normalized search interest, 24 months of history, related terms, popular pins; Pinterest cited as about 631M monthly users. Good for visual/planning categories (home, decor, gifting, wedding, fashion). — [DoDropshipping tool list](https://dodropshipping.com/best-product-research-tools/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/pinterest-trends-tool-guide); [ImgSEO](https://imgseo.io/blog/pinterest-trends-product-research-guide)
- Minea: free "Lite" plan with 200 credits, email signup only, no card, no time limit; includes Meta ads access, Chrome extension, basic filters; NO TikTok/Pinterest access on free. Paid: Starter $49/mo (10k credits), Premium $99/mo (100k), Business $399/mo (150k); annual billing about 30% off. (Third-party affiliate pricing pages; verify on minea.com.) — [AmzNinja](https://amz.ninja/minea-pricing/); [AffMaven](https://affmaven.com/minea-pricing/); [DoDropshipping Minea review](https://dodropshipping.com/minea-review/)
- PiPiAds: credit-based since "2.0"; reported free trial of 500 credits (as of Aug 9, 2026), 0 detail views, no card required; 1 credit = basic data point, 20 credits = detailed analysis; free view hides ad details, country, CTA, e-commerce platform. A separate $1/3-day full-access trial is also reported. — [AffMaven](https://affmaven.com/pipiads-free-trial/); [WinningHunter](https://winninghunter.com/insights/pipiads-review/); [Minea competitor page](https://www.minea.com/competitor-analysis/competitor-spy-tools/pipiads) (competitor; conflicting trial info across sources)
- Dropship.io: no permanent free plan; trial reported as 3 days with $1 card authorization (some sources say 7 days). Basic $39/mo ($26 annual), Standard $59/mo, Premium $99/mo; Basic limits Product Database searches to 20/day. — [Dropship.io pricing](https://www.dropship.io/pricing); [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/dropship-io/free-trial); [ProductLair](https://productlair.com/dropship-io-pricing)
- CJ Dropshipping: free account, no monthly fee (pay product + shipping per order); free tier reported as 5 daily sourcing requests and unlimited listings; paid memberships Plus $15.99, Prime $19.99, Advanced $59.99/mo. Its trending/"hot product" listing details were not confirmed in the results. — [RevenueGeeks CJ review](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/cjdropshipping); [AutoDS comparison](https://www.autods.com/blog/dropshipping-tips-strategies/subscription-costs-comparison-autods-cjdropshipping-zendrop/)
- Spocket: 14-day free trial; paid plans from about $24-$40/mo up to $99; "free tier too limited for active selling" (third-party). Its value is US/EU suppliers with faster shipping. — [Ecommerce Paradise](https://ecommerceparadise.com/spocket-vs-cjdropshipping/); [Teemdrop](https://teemdrop.com/blogs/best-cj-dropshipping-alternatives-2026)
- Amazon Best Sellers/Movers & Shakers, AliExpress trending, Reddit: no 2026 official-page verification done in this research (searches did not return primary pages). Usage is standard practice but listed here as UNSOURCED.

### Inferences
- A zero-cost research stack: Google Trends (5-year view + seasonality + regional interest) -> Pinterest Trends and TikTok Creative Center Top Products (what is being advertised/trending) -> Meta Ad Library (are multiple advertisers running ads for the same product, how long since "started running") -> Amazon Best Sellers reviews (complaints = product-improvement angle) -> Reddit (real pain-point language). Paid spy tools are optional and their free tiers are too small to matter.
- Beware that "advertised on TikTok/Meta" can mean the product is already saturated; absence of sustained ads can mean either no demand or untested opportunity.

### Gaps
- Official pricing/free-tier pages for Minea, PiPiAds, Dropship.io, Spocket, CJ were not readable (proxy block), so details come from affiliate/review sites that may be outdated or biased.
- Amazon Best Sellers, AliExpress trending, Reddit tools: not sourced.

## 3. Validating demand for free before buying inventory

### Takeaway
Sources agree on a stack of cheap signals: search/trend data, competitor ad longevity, organic content engagement, and a pre-order/waitlist landing page; but the quantitative thresholds that circulate (for example "500+ qualified signups in 4-8 weeks") come from generic startup/waitlist content, not dropshipping-specific evidence. True demand validation is a purchase, so a live "pre-order/sell first" test with honest delivery times is the strongest free test.

### Cited Findings
- Waitlist/pre-order pages validate demand before production/stock; benchmark cited: 500+ qualified signups in 4-8 weeks with modest promotion indicates demand (generic startup advice, not dropshipping-specific, not empirically validated). Free waitlist tools exist (for example LaunchList). — [LaunchList](https://getlaunchlist.com/waitlist-for-ecommerce); [Ship With AI Substack](https://shipwithai.substack.com/p/5-steps-to-validate-market-demand); [Unicorn Platform](https://unicornplatform.com/blog/how-to-launch-a-waitlist-and-build-hype-for-your-product/)
- Competitor ad longevity in Meta Ad Library: "focus on ads running longest, they are usually profitable"; but the library has no spend filter or run-length signal and commercial archives vanish when the campaign ends. — [Zik Analytics](https://www.zikanalytics.com/academy/shopify/tips-and-tricks-to-increase-sales-shopify/facebook-ad-library-how-to-use-meta-ad-library-to-find-winning-ads); [AdLibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/best-meta-ad-library-alternatives-for-dropshipping-2026) (competitor vendor)
- Google Trends caveat: relative index only, so use it for direction and comparison, not for sizing. — [Glimpse](https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/advantages-disadvantages/)
- Honest-experiment content: YouTubers' "I tried dropshipping for 24 hours / 7 / 14 days" videos exist but are short windows (many are course funnels). Ecom King (Kamil Sattar) reportedly lost "a few thousand dollars" in year one before reaching $1M+ revenue (he sells education; survivorship). — [YouTube search results](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ywI5vAe2FmA); [Shopify blog on Ecom King](https://shopify.com/blog/ecom-king-dropshipping)
- I did not find a credible, data-backed study of organic TikTok/Reels product tests or polls as predictors of dropshipping sales.

### Inferences
- Suggested free validation ladder (my synthesis, not a sourced method): (1) trend and keyword evidence; (2) 3+ independent advertisers running the product for weeks in Meta Ad Library, in the target country; (3) read negative reviews on Amazon/AliExpress/Reddit to confirm a real recurring problem; (4) post 10-20 organic short videos or Reddit/Facebook-group value posts and measure saves/comments/link clicks (profile-link clicks and DMs are stronger than views); (5) a one-page store or pre-order/waitlist page with clear lead time and a Stripe/PayPal checkout; count real orders (even 1-3 genuine purchases beat 100 likes). Note: taking money for items you cannot deliver is a legal/platform-policy risk; use clear delivery windows and fulfil via supplier order after purchase (that is standard dropshipping).
- Polls (Reddit, Facebook groups) measure stated interest, which is weak evidence; use for language/objection discovery rather than demand sizing.

### Gaps
- No empirical conversion-from-organic-test benchmarks for dropshipping found.
- Reddit r/dropship / r/ecommerce threads could not be read (fetch blocked; search returned mostly vendor/blog pages).

## 4. Viable vs saturated/high-risk niches for beginners in 2025-2026 (with contrary evidence)

### Takeaway
Practitioner/vendor consensus: generic gadgets, phone accessories, LED lights, posture correctors, apparel, jewelry, supplements, mainstream electronics, and branded/character merchandise are crowded or risky; "narrow audience + specific problem + no brand" micro-niches (for example arthritis-friendly tools, pet problem-solvers, hobby-specific accessories) are recommended. But nearly all of this is from tool vendors and educators with something to sell, and there is little hard data on actual niche profitability.

### Cited Findings
- "Avoid" list: high-return products (clothing), IP-risk items (anime/branded/replicas), oversaturated "guru products", low-ticket thin-margin items, fragile/bulky goods, regulated products such as supplements. — [ScaleOrder (practitioner-vendor)](https://scaleorder.com/blogs/i-tested-6-winning-niches-heres-why-they-failed-dropshipping-niches-to-avoid-in-2026/); [Ship To The Moon](https://www.shiptothemoon.com/11-most-profitable-dropshipping-niches-and-5-to-avoid/); [NicheDropshipping](https://nichedropshipping.com/saturated-dropshipping-niches/)
- Supplements: FDA/EU compliance, liability, shelf life; one source claims "highest dropship saturation (7.3%)" (unverified metric). Electronics: high competition, price wars, and 8-15% return rates. — [Search summary across the above](https://www.fforder.com/post/profitable-dropshipping-niches-in-2026)
- Dropbuild (a store-builder vendor claiming a view of 26,500+ stores): a general store selling phone cases, LED lights, posture correctors competes with thousands of identical shops; a narrow store (ergonomic tools for arthritis patients, natural skincare for eczema) faces different competition; failure is driven more by lack of differentiation than by overall saturation. Self-reported, unverified data; vendor selling a builder. — [Dropbuild](https://www.dropbuild.com/blog/is-dropshipping-oversaturated)
- "Recommended" niches: smart-home gadgets, men's grooming, fragrance/cosmetics (repeat purchase + giftability), pet products (low returns), problem-solving health/household items, micro-niches by hobby. — [Sell The Trend](https://www.sellthetrend.com/blog/best-dropshipping-niches); [FFOrder](https://www.fforder.com/post/profitable-dropshipping-niches-in-2026); [Neonjelly](https://neonjelly.io/blog/best-dropshipping-niches-2026)
- Competition from Temu/Shein/Amazon: "never compete with Temu, SHEIN or Amazon on cheap products"; Temu is not viable as a supplier (no API, branded packaging). — [Branvas Temu](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/temu-dropshipping-risks-alternatives); [Extuitive](https://extuitive.com/articles/temu-dropshipping)
- Contrary evidence: Spocket, CJ, AutoDS, Dropbuild (all tool vendors) argue dropshipping "is not dead" and that supplements/electronics/beauty can work with branding and local fulfilment; list pieces in "best niche" posts frequently include the same categories others list as saturated (for example health/wellness and beauty show the highest conversion rates in benchmarks: health and wellness about 3.3%, beauty about 2.6-4.9%). — [Spocket](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/is-dropshipping-dead-in-2026); [Growthsuite](https://www.growthsuite.net/resources/shopify-conversion-rate/complete-guide/benchmarks-by-industry)
- Post-de-minimis shift: sources argue the surviving model is local-warehouse (US/EU) suppliers or private label, and "dropshipping now goes local". — [Syncee](https://syncee.com/blog/drop-shipping/de-minimis-exemption-is-over/)

### Inferences
- Beauty/health have high conversion but heavy regulation/claims risk (ad-policy and compliance, especially supplements and anything with health claims); not "free" for a beginner.
- Niche picking from "best niche" lists is circular (the same lists feed everyone); a better differentiator is a specific sub-audience and problem you can talk to credibly, ideally one you can create content for in your own language/markets.
- Credible independent practitioner voices (Ecommerce Fuel, r/dropship threads, named YouTubers with disclosed P&Ls) were not retrievable; their views are therefore missing from this note.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) practitioner survey or disclosed-P&L dataset by niche.
- No verified per-niche profitability numbers.

## 5. Single-product vs niche vs general store in 2026

### Takeaway
Most 2026 content recommends a niche store for beginners; one-product stores are described as fast to set up but fragile and low-trust; general stores as unfocused and now hit hardest by shipping/brand expectations. All evidence is opinion from vendors/educators; no controlled comparison found.

### Cited Findings
- Niche stores: "perfect balance", typically higher conversion than general stores, more trust, easier to scale, higher margins when selling multiple items in one category. — [DoDropshipping comparison](https://dodropshipping.com/general-vs-niche-dropshipping-store/); [Inventory Source](https://www.inventorysource.com/general-vs-niche-vs-one-product-dropshipping-store/); [DropSure](https://www.dropsure.com/blog/niche-store-or-general-store-which-path-should-beginners-choose-in-2026/)
- One-product stores: faster to build, easier to optimise ads, but no upsell/cross-sell, one failure kills the business, and some buyers find them "too good to be true". — [Dropshipping Hustle](https://www.dropshippinghustle.com/one-vs-multiple-products/); [Dropship Lifestyle](https://www.dropshiplifestyle.com/one-product-dropshipping-store/)
- General stores: stable (something for everyone), can pivot to whatever trends, but need constant work and struggle with trust, branding and shipping expectations; Dropbuild lists generic stores as the main crowding case. — [Inventory Source](https://www.inventorysource.com/general-vs-niche-vs-one-product-dropshipping-store/); [Dropbuild](https://www.dropbuild.com/blog/is-dropshipping-oversaturated)

### Inferences
- A pragmatic hybrid for zero budget: a narrow niche brand (3-10 related products) built around one "hero" product that is being tested through content; this keeps the trust/AOV advantages of a niche while concentrating effort. This is my synthesis, not a sourced finding.

### Gaps
- No data comparing success rates across store models.

## 6. Honest base rate of success/failure for new dropshippers

### Takeaway
There is no reliable primary base-rate study. The widely repeated "80-90% fail" figure has no traceable original source, and "success" is defined inconsistently; treat it as a rough prior consistent with small-business failure plus a near-zero barrier to entry, not a measured statistic.

### Cited Findings
- Commonly quoted: 80-90% of new dropshipping businesses fail/quit within the first year; 10-20% generate recurring profit; 1-5% build scalable brands; about 1.5% of stores exceed $50K/month revenue; "about 10% succeed in the first year". All from vendor/blog aggregators. — [TrueProfit](https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-success-rate); [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/is-dropshipping-profitable); [Launchese](https://blog.launchese.com/the-hidden-truth-why-90-of-dropshippers-fail-and-how-smart-entrepreneurs-beat-the-odds/); [Printful stats](https://www.printful.com/blog/dropshipping-statistics)
- Provenance problem: search of "90% failure" sources found no specific original study; the number is repeated across YouTube/Reddit/blogs; stats usually count abandoned stores that were never seriously operated; some sources give 10-30% success (70-90% failure). — [DoDropshipping stats](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-statistics/); [Rovela](https://rovela.ai/blog/dropshipping-failure-rate); [Ecommerce Paradise](https://ecommerceparadise.com/how-many-dropshippers-fail-the-real-statistics-and-how-to-beat-the-odds/)

### Inferences
- A sensible planning prior is "most first attempts do not become profitable"; with zero budget, the realistic early goal is to find evidence of demand and run a few cheap experiments rather than expect a profitable store in the first months.
- Revenue figures (like "$40K in 90 days") are revenue, not profit, and are not base rates.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed, Shopify, or government-sourced base rate of dropshipping store survival/profitability.
- The original source of the 80-90% failure figure was not found.
