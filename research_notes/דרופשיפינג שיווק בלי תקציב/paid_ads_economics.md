## 1. 2025-2026 ad benchmarks (Meta, TikTok), conversion rates, ROAS, and by region

### Takeaway
Meta ecommerce CPM is roughly $13-17 (US higher, ~$16-23 depending on dataset; UK/CA/AU ~$10-12 or up to $18 for AU; Germany ~$9-10), CTR ~1.9-2.7%, CPC ~$0.57-0.87, and site conversion rate ~1.4-1.5%. TikTok CPM is cheaper on paper ($4.80-13), CPC $0.74-1.50, but TikTok CVR is similar or lower. CPM has been rising ~20% YoY, and Q4 CPMs spike by 50%+. Typical dropshipping ROAS is ~2.0, which is at or below the break-even ROAS for most dropshipping margins (see section 2).

### Inferences
- **[MY CALC]** Implied CPA from medians: CPM $15.06 and CTR 2.39% give CPC ≈ $0.63; at CVR 1.53% this gives CPA ≈ $41. Using the Skale median (CPC $0.87, CVR ~1.5%) gives ≈ $58. So a beginner store with a median-quality funnel pays roughly $40-60 per purchase on Meta cold traffic. That makes sub-$50 AOV products structurally unprofitable on paid ads (see section 2).
- The "ROAS 2.96" DTC median is from established brands with email/retargeting/repeat purchases; a new single-product store should expect lower first-order ROAS.
- Because three datasets disagree by up to 40% on US CPM, the planning rule should be "assume US CPM $15-25, UK/CA/AU $10-18, DE/most EU $9-13" and verify with your own account within the first $100-200 of spend.

## 2. Break-even ROAS formula, worked examples, and required margin/AOV

### Takeaway
Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin (before ads), where contribution margin = (AOV − product cost − shipping − payment fees − expected refunds/chargebacks − per-order app/other costs) ÷ AOV. Typical dropshipping contribution margins of 25-45% imply break-even ROAS of 2.2-4.0 — right at or above the ~2.0 average ROAS. A beginner needs AOV ≥ ~$60-80 and contribution margin ≥ ~45-50% (before ads) for paid ads to have a realistic chance on cold traffic.

### Cited Findings
- Break-even ROAS = 1 / contribution margin (after COGS, payment and platform fees, expected refunds) — [Skaleit](https://skaleit.agency/blog/break-even-roas-calculator-formula-target-roas/), [Gentool Lab](https://gentoollab.com/guides/how-to-calculate-break-even-roas/)
- Example from a calculator article: 60% gross margin, 5% fees, 10% refund rate leaves ~45% of each sale, not 60% — [Doba](https://www.doba.com/blog/marketing-and-sales-growth/conversion-strategies/how-to-calculate-break-even-roas-for-dropshipping-campaigns-in-2025-38921)
- Dropshipping gross margins commonly cited as 15-30% (after product and shipping); ROAS 3x often cited as minimum; a 25%-margin business "might need 4:1 ROAS to break even" — [Doba](https://www.doba.com/blog/marketing-and-sales-growth/conversion-strategies/how-to-calculate-break-even-roas-for-dropshipping-campaigns-in-2025-38921), [Rule1](https://rule1.ai/articles/roas-benchmarks)
- Payment fees: Shopify Payments/Stripe-style 2.9% + $0.30 in the US; PayPal 2.99% + $0.49 domestic, 3.49% + $0.49 for PayPal Checkout; cross-border adds ~1.5%; currency-conversion spread 3-4% — "close to 8%" worst-case total for an international payment — [CheckoutPage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/paypal-fees), [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/us/blog/how-much-does-paypal-business-charge-for-international-payments)
- Chargeback thresholds: Stripe 0.5%, PayPal and Shopify ~1% monthly; Visa VAMP merchant "Excessive" threshold lowered to 1.5% from April 2026; reserve of 20% for ≥120 days possible; dropshipping chargeback rates run higher than ecommerce average of 0.47-0.95%; target <0.3% — [Chargeflow](https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/chargeback-threshold-limits), [Chargebacks911](https://chargebacks911.com/chargeback-types/shopify-chargebacks/shopify-chargeback-limit/)

### Worked examples **[MY CALC]** (assumptions: Shopify-Payments-like fee 2.9% + $0.30; refunds/chargebacks 5% of revenue; apps/other $0.50-1/order; "product cost" = supplier price; shipping paid to supplier/agent; no tariffs)

| | A: low-ticket gadget | B: mid-ticket | C: higher-ticket / bundle |
|---|---|---|---|
| AOV | $30 | $50 | $80 |
| Product cost | $10 | $15 | $20 |
| Shipping cost | $5 | $6 | $8 |
| Payment fee (2.9% + $0.30) | $1.17 | $1.75 | $2.62 |
| Refund/chargeback allowance (5%) | $1.50 | $2.50 | $4.00 |
| Apps/other | $0.50 | $0.75 | $1.00 |
| **Contribution before ads** | **$11.83 (39%)** | **$23.99 (48%)** | **$44.38 (55%)** |
| **Break-even ROAS** | **2.54** | **2.08** | **1.80** |
| **Break-even CPA** (max you can pay per order) | **$11.83** | **$23.99** | **$44.38** |
| vs. implied median Meta CPA $41-58 | loses $30-46/order | loses $17-34 | ≈ break-even to −$14 |

- With PayPal/international fees (~4.5% + $0.49 + FX 3% on conversion) the contribution in A drops by ~$2.5 more, B by ~$3.9, C by ~$6.3, raising break-even ROAS accordingly (for B: 1/(23.99−3.9)/50 ≈ 2.49).
- Net profit per order = contribution − CPA. At target CPA of 70% of break-even CPA, B yields ≈ $7 profit/order (14%).
- **Rule of thumb [MY CALC]:** need break-even CPA ≥ ~$35-45 on cold Meta/TikTok traffic with a ~1.5% CVR and ~$0.60-0.90 CPC, i.e., contribution before ads ≥ ~$40 per order. This implies AOV of roughly $70-100 with 45-55% contribution margin, or a ~$50 AOV with ≥70% contribution (unusual for AliExpress-sourced goods). Order bumps, bundles ("buy 2 get 1"), and upsells are the practical way to lift AOV.
- Add: Shopify plan ($29/mo basic; I did not verify current price), domain, theme, email apps are fixed costs not in per-order math. Not sourced in this note — **[UNCERTAIN]**.

### Inferences
- Most sub-$40 AOV, "$10-15 gadget" dropshipping products cannot pay for cold-traffic paid acquisition at average CPMs. This is the single most important filter for a beginner deciding between organic and paid.
- Break-even ROAS above ~2.5 on a thin-margin product is not achievable at the ~2.0 industry average, matching the profitability data in section 7.

### Gaps
- No primary source for refund rates specific to dropshipping (the 5% above is an assumption). Chargebacks are cited only by vendor blogs.
- Current Shopify plan prices/transaction fees for third-party gateways (2% extra when not using Shopify Payments — not verified) were not researched.

## 3. Meta and TikTok best practices (2025-2026), test budgets and frameworks

### Takeaway
Meta's own guidance is creative-led: broad targeting, Advantage+ Sales campaigns, fewer campaigns/ad sets, many meaningfully different creatives (Andromeda retrieval). Practitioners commonly quote $20-50/day per product for testing, but the learning-phase math (~50 events/week) shows this is too small to optimize on purchases at typical CPAs. A more honest minimum is a fixed test pool of ~2-3x break-even CPA per creative cluster, or optimizing on upstream events for the first test.

### Cited Findings
**Meta**
- Meta (April 2025 post as summarised) says the main lever for finding the right audience has shifted from narrow targeting to creative diversification; Meta internal test: 1 ad set with 25 diverse creatives gave 17% more conversions at 16% lower cost vs 5 ad sets × 5 creatives; Advantage+ creative drove a "22% ROAS lift" (Meta-reported figures quoted by vendors) — [Chatterbuzz Media](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/), [Logical Position](https://www.logicalposition.com/blog/the-2026-paid-social-playbook), [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)
- Recommended structure 2026: fewer campaigns/ad sets, more creatives per ad set, broad targeting, all placements; minimum ~6 meaningfully different ads per ad set, 15-20 active creatives for well-performing accounts; variations must differ in concept/hook/format, not colour — [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/metas-2026-algorithm-update-what-andromeda-changed-and-how-to-adapt-your-ads/), [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026) (agency blogs; Meta claims are second-hand)
- Learning phase: ~50 optimization events in 7 days per ad set; significant edits (budget, targeting, creative, optimization) reset it; "Learning Limited" when Meta expects <50 — [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/what-is-the-meta-ads-learning-phase-how-to-exit-it-faster-in-2026/), [Digital Ring](https://thedigitalring.com/insights/meta-ads-learning-phase-what-advertisers-need-to-know) (the Meta Help page itself not read)
- Test budgets quoted: $20-50/day per product with at least 50 purchases before scaling; $20/day floor; a 2% CVR and $14.40 CPM imply ~$35-50/day to reliably exit learning; ≥3 creative variations per product; $20-50 per product over 2-3 days with Advantage+ Shopping — [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/best-way-to-test-products-on-facebook-ads-for-dropshipping), [AutoDS](https://www.autods.com/blog/initial-marketing-budget-for-dropshipping/), [Dropshipping Champions](https://dropshippingchampions.com/blog/facebook-ads-for-dropshipping) (vendor/tool blogs; [GURU/VENDOR])
- Per-product test budgets: $210-$490 per product; 5-10 products tested before a winner, costing $1,000-5,000; hit rate 5-10% blind vs 20-30% for pre-validated products; most experienced dropshippers use $200-500 per product — [Dropship Seek](https://dropshipseek.com/blog/how-to-test-dropshipping-products-2026), [HulkApps](https://www.hulkapps.com/blogs/ecommerce-hub/how-many-products-to-test-before-finding-a-winner-in-dropshipping), [AI Store Builder](https://www.aistorebuilder.com/blog/how-to-test-products-dropshipping) (these hit-rate numbers are unsourced assertions by vendors; **[UNCERTAIN]**)

**TikTok**
- Official/commonly quoted minimums: campaign-level daily budget $50, ad-group-level $20 (as quoted by vendors; not verified on TikTok help page); practical Smart+ minimum $100-200/day per vendor; SME test €300-500 over two weeks (€30-50/day) using Spark Ads on best organic video — [Admanage](https://admanage.ai/blog/tiktok-ads-cost), [Adintime](https://adintime.com/en/blog/tiktok-ads-the-complete-guide-to-advertising-on-tiktok-in-2026-n311), [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/how-much-does-tiktok-advertising-cost-in-2026)
- Smart+ is TikTok's automation layer (equivalent to Advantage+); Smart+ with Lowest Cost bidding the suggested starting point; 3-5 creatives per ad group, refresh often; Spark Ads boost organic posts/creator posts for trust and lower CPA — [Social Champ](https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/tiktok-ads/), [Enrich Labs Spark guide](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-complete-guide-2026), [TikAdTools](https://tikadtools.com/blog/tiktok-ads-best-practices/)

### Inferences
- **[MY CALC]** 50 purchases/week at CPA $30 = $1,500/week ≈ $214/day; at CPA $41 ≈ $293/day. A $20-50/day budget cannot exit learning on purchase optimization for one ad set; those budgets give roughly 0.5-1.5 purchases/day at best at CPA $30-40. This is why many "$20/day test" results are statistically noise.
- Practical beginner approach given a small bankroll: (a) skip precise statistical testing, treat tests as "kill/continue" signals using upstream metrics (CTR, hook rate, cost per add-to-cart/initiate checkout, thumb-stop), (b) cap the spend per creative cluster at ~2-3x break-even CPA with no purchases = kill, (c) get cheap creatives first (section 6) because under Andromeda the creative is the targeting.
- The Advantage+ vs manual debate: Meta-reported gains are self-reported; independent confirmation not found.

### Gaps
- Meta's and TikTok's own help-centre pages (minimum budgets, learning phase, Smart+ specifics) were not fetchable; claims are quoted second-hand.
- No disclosed-data practitioner thread (e.g., a verified P&L) with test-budget results; the "guru" corpus returned only gumroad/Fiverr sales pages.
- Whether Andromeda changes the best budget for a $0-$500/month account: no source.

## 4. Platform policy risks (Meta, TikTok, Google, payment processors) and Israel specifics

### Takeaway
Dropshipping itself is not banned on Meta/TikTok/Google, but the usual dropshipper behaviours (undisclosed long shipping, fake scarcity, mismatched "free shipping", unauthorised stock images/brand claims, re-creating accounts after a ban) cause rejections and bans; enforcement tightened in 2025-2026. Israel adds two structural issues: Stripe and Shopify Payments are not available to Israeli-registered businesses, and PayPal's cross-border cost is high.

### Cited Findings
**Meta**
- "Circumventing systems" (opening a new account after a disable) is a critical, very-low-recoverability violation and can lead to permanent bans — [Grow With Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-account-disabled/), [Audit Socials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ad-account-disabled-recovery-guide-2026)
- Landing-page mismatches like "free shipping" in the ad but a minimum purchase on the page account for ~11% of 2026 rejections (vendor statistic; **[UNCERTAIN]**) — [Scaling Journal](https://scalingjournal.com/meta-advertising-policies-explained/), [1ClickReport](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/meta-ad-policy-changes-2026-compliance-guide)
- Meta's GEM rollout (Nov 2025, per vendor) made enforcement scan creative, landing page and behaviour signals; untrustworthy sites, misleading ads and slow shipping get flagged — [1ClickReport](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/meta-ad-policy-changes-2026-compliance-guide), [DoDropshipping](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-marketplace-bans/)
- Payment methods for Israel-based Meta advertisers: credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx in accepted currencies) and PayPal; currency/country/time zone are locked on account creation and determine billing/taxes — [Meta Business Help: accepted payment options](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/212763688755026), [Agrowth](https://agrowth.io/blogs/facebook-ads/facebook-ad-account-currency)

**TikTok**
- Ads/shop must disclose product origin and realistic delivery times; before/after images banned; no soliciting positive reviews; fake tracking prohibited; permanent advertiser ban on third violation with no appeal (vendor summary; **[UNCERTAIN]** on exact enforcement mechanics) — [Audit Socials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-dropshipping-ecommerce-ad-compliance-2026), [Shoplazza](https://www.shoplazza.com/blog/tiktok-shop-policy-update), [LZ Dropshipping](https://www.lzdropshipping.com/tiktok-shop-policy-updates-2026-what-dropshippers-need-to-know-to-avoid-bans/) (the last two partly TikTok Shop, not TikTok Ads)
- TikTok Ads Manager is available to Israel-based advertisers; customers in Israel must self-assess VAT and issue self-billing Israeli tax invoices quarterly to TikTok; billing options prepay/auto-pay/monthly invoicing — [TikTok Ads: Israel taxes](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/israel-taxes?lang=en), [TikTok billing options](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-billing-options)

**Google**
- Compliant dropshipping is allowed on Shopping, but Merchant Center "Misrepresentation" suspensions often hit new dropshipping stores (unclear/understated shipping times the top cause, missing return policy, ad-page mismatch); suspension is issued without warning; limited appeals — [GMC Check](https://gmccheck.com/google-ads/dropshipping-ads), [Dropified](https://www.dropified.com/blog/google-ads-for-dropshipping/), [Icomchain](https://www.icomchain.com/google-merchant-center-misrepresentation-suspension-the-complete-2026-recovery-guide-30-questions-answered/) (vendors; [GURU/VENDOR])

**Payments / Israel**
- Shopify Payments does not operate in Israel; PayPal Express was the only Shopify-subscription gateway available in Israel per one Israeli Shopify app support page; local gateways (Tranzila, CardCom, PayMe, Allpay) mainly serve domestic ILS — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/c/payments-shipping-and/shopify-payments-for-stores-based-in-israel/td-p/977136), [Ongoing Payments](https://intercom.help/ongoingpayments/en/articles/8105193-ongoing-shopify-subscriptions-for-israel-which-payment-gateways-can-i-use), [Allpay app](https://apps.shopify.com/allpay-secure-payment), [Cart DNA](https://www.cartdna.com/shopify-payment-guide/middle-east/israel)
- Stripe does not support businesses registered in Israel; workaround is a US LLC (e.g., via Stripe Atlas) with US bank account; Stripe has been struggling to get an Israeli clearing licence — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-stripe-struggling-to-receive-israeli-clearing-license-1001381078), [Adir CPA](https://adircpa.com/en/guides/stripe-israel-tax), [Startglobal](https://startglobal.co/us/international/israel/stripe/), [Doola](https://www.doola.com/stripe-guide/how-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-israel/) (the LLC route has tax/legal implications; get a CPA; **not legal/tax advice**)
- Dropshipping merchants face higher odds of Shopify reserve holds / mandatory dispute enrollment; Visa VAMP threshold dropped to 1.5% in April 2026; 20% rolling reserve for ≥120 days is a common processor response — [DoDropshipping Payments](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-payments/), [Chargeflow](https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/chargeback-threshold-limits)

### Inferences
- For a Hebrew-speaking beginner in Israel, the ad-billing side is workable (cards, USD billing — currency choice at ad-account creation is permanent; choosing USD avoids ILS/USD conversion mismatch for USD-priced stores [UNCERTAIN: no source confirms USD is selectable for Israel accounts]), but the store payment side is the bottleneck and a hidden cost: PayPal + FX can cost ~5-8% (section 2). A US LLC + Stripe/Shopify Payments route lowers fees but adds legal/tax complexity.
- Because VAT/self-billing rules for Israel apply to TikTok and probably Meta, ad spend may carry tax administration (e.g., self-assessment of reverse-charge VAT) — Meta's Israel treatment not found. **Check with an Israeli accountant before spending.**
- Ban risk is a binary risk to a bootstrapped beginner: one disabled ad account/ payment hold can freeze working capital. Hence: honest shipping times, no fake urgency, real return policy before the first ad dollar.

### Gaps
- Meta's Israel-specific tax/billing treatment and whether Israel accounts can choose USD: not found.
- Any ad-account issues specific to Israel-based advertisers (e.g., conflict-related content moderation, sanctions screening): not researched; no source.
- Primary policy pages (Meta Advertising Standards, TikTok Advertising Policies, Google Misrepresentation policy) not read directly.

## 5. Google Shopping / Performance Max / YouTube viability for beginners

### Takeaway
Google Shopping can be one of the better-converting channels (high intent) but is gated by Merchant Center compliance, price-competition against established sellers, and the need to wait for conversion data before PMax. YouTube/Demand Gen is a scaling channel, not a starting point.

### Cited Findings
- Google Shopping is described as high-ROI when set up compliantly; for beginners "start with Manual CPC Shopping, collect 20-30 conversions, then PMax as stage 3" — [Dropified](https://www.dropified.com/blog/google-ads-for-dropshipping/), [AB Ecom](https://ab-ecom.com/google-ads/google-ads-for-dropshipping/) ([GURU/VENDOR])
- Misrepresentation triggers: understated shipping time, missing return policy, ad/page mismatch — [GMC Check](https://gmccheck.com/google-ads/dropshipping-ads)
- YouTube: CPV $0.02-0.10 (ecommerce avg $0.024 skippable) or $5-10 CPM; dropshipping-specific CPV $0.05-0.15; ≥$1,500/month recommended; official minimum $10/day — [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/youtube-ads-cost/), [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/youtube-ads-cost-guide), [AI Advantage Agency](https://aiadvantageagency.com/youtube-ads-cost-for-ecommerce-cpv-cpm-and-roas-benchmarks-2026/)

## 7. Honest profitability: success rates, margins, tariffs and de minimis

### Takeaway
Credible numbers on dropshipping profitability do not exist in a single audited dataset; vendor blogs variously claim 10-20% of stores are profitable, 80-90% fail in year one, or "1-5% real success". Typical net margins quoted: beginners <10%, experienced 15-20%. The end of US de minimis (29 Aug 2025) and the new EU €3 duty (1 July 2026) removed the cost advantage of China-direct shipping for thin-margin products.

### Cited Findings
- 10-20% of stores profitable consistently; 80-90% of new businesses fail in year one; "real success rate ~1-5%"; net margins 15-25% for healthy stores, 15-20% experienced, beginners <10% or <15%; gross margins 65-70% for healthy stores (this conflicts with the 15-30% gross margins quoted for product-after-shipping in section 2: definitions differ, "gross margin" often excludes shipping/fees) — [TrueProfit](https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-success-rate), [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/how-profitable-is-dropshipping), [Dropbuild (claims data from "26,500+ stores"; methodology unseen)](https://www.dropbuild.com/blog/is-dropshipping-still-profitable), [Sellers Commerce](https://www.sellerscommerce.com/blog/dropshipping-statistics/). All are vendor blogs; no source I could read documents a methodology. **[UNCERTAIN / GURU-VENDOR]**
- US de minimis exemption ended for all countries 29 Aug 2025 (after China/HK on May 2025); all parcels face duties; one summary says a 10% baseline + 34% China tariff implies up to a 54% cost increase [UNCERTAIN: rates changed repeatedly in 2025-2026 and I did not verify current rates]; China-direct dropshipping margins "compressed from 15-20% to single digits"; daily duty-free package volume fell from ~4M to ~600K — [Syncee](https://syncee.com/blog/drop-shipping/de-minimis-exemption-is-over/), [Syncee tariffs](https://syncee.com/blog/syncee-news/how-us-tariffs-affect-dropshipping/), [TopDawg](https://topdawg.com/blog/aliexpress-dropshipping-in-trouble-why-retailers-are-moving-to-us-suppliers-in-2025) (the last is a US-supplier platform with an interest; [GURU/VENDOR])
- EU: €3 flat customs duty on parcels <€150 from outside the EU, applies from 1 July 2026, temporary until 1 July 2028 — [WWD](https://wwd.com/business-news/government-trade/eu-3-euro-fee-small-parcels-targeting-shein-temu-1238425043/), [Baker Tilly SEE](https://bakertillysee.com/insights/major-eu-customs-update-e3-duty-on-low-value-e-commerce-imports-starting-july-2026/), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/tech/eu-taxes-low-value-parcels-aliexpress-shein-temu/); one analyst expects limited slowdown for Temu/Shein — [Shopappy](https://shopappy.com/trade/cross-border-commerce/eu-de-minimis-wont-slow-temu-shein)

### Inferences
- A €3 duty on a $10 item is 30% of product price; for a $15-product B example in section 2 it drops contribution from $24 to ~$20.5 and raises break-even ROAS from 2.08 to ~2.4 **[MY CALC]**.
- Even taking the most optimistic vendor numbers (15-20% net margins for experienced operators), a beginner running ads at ROAS ~2 with 40-50% contribution margins is near zero profit. Real success is therefore dominated by AOV/margin/creative skill, not by "winning products."
- Distinguish guru claims (course sellers, product-spy tool vendors: e.g., the "winning product" gumroad/Fiverr listings that dominated my search) from data: none of the success-rate figures above is traceable to a primary study.

## 8. Staged budget plan: when and how to graduate from organic to paid ($0 / $100 / $500 / $1,000 per month)

### Takeaway
Graduate to paid only after four gates are passed (below). Spend nothing on ads until the product clears the unit-economics test (break-even CPA ≥ ~$35-45) and the store has trust/shipping/policy basics; before then spend on creative and tools. This plan is my synthesis **[MY CALC / inference]** built on the cited data; budget thresholds are not an established standard.

### Cited Findings (inputs to the plan)
- Practitioner test-budget claims: $20-50/day per product; $200-500 per product; $1,000-5,000 to find the first winner; 5-10% blind hit rate — see section 3 sources (vendor claims, [UNCERTAIN]).
- Learning-phase requirement 50 events/week — [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/what-is-the-meta-ads-learning-phase-how-to-exit-it-faster-in-2026/)
- UGC at $75-300/video; Billo ~$99 — [PPC.io](https://ppc.io/blog/ugc-pricing)
- TikTok SME test €300-500 over two weeks — [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/how-much-does-tiktok-advertising-cost-in-2026), [Adintime](https://adintime.com/en/blog/tiktok-ads-the-complete-guide-to-advertising-on-tiktok-in-2026-n311)

### Graduation gates (all four before ad spend) **[MY CALC / inference]**
1. Contribution before ads ≥ $35-45/order (AOV ≳ $60-80) after fees/refunds/tariffs; break-even ROAS ≤ ~2.2.
2. Store trust basics: real shipping times/origin disclosed, return policy, contact details, policy pages (needed for Meta/TikTok/Google compliance, section 4).
3. Payment processing that won't freeze funds (PayPal/Allpay or LLC+Stripe decided; chargeback plan).
4. Organic signal: ≥1 organic video/post with above-average view-through or saves/comments, or ≥ a few organic sales/ pre-orders confirming the offer — and ≥5 distinct creatives ready.

### Stages **[MY CALC / inference, not a sourced standard]**
- **$0/month:** organic content (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), product gifting to nano creators (cost = COGS + shipping), validate demand with organic traffic and landing-page tests, sample the product yourself, build 5-10 raw hooks. Goal: prove the offer converts and learn what creative works. No ads.
- **$100/month:** buy 1 UGC pack (e.g., 1 video ≈ $99 on Billo) or 2-3 beginner creators ($75-150 each), or Spark-boost the best organic post at ~$3-5/day. Treat the money as creative budget, not traffic budget. Expected: 0-3 purchases; it is too small for conversion-optimised learning (a $100 spend at median CPA $41 buys ~2.4 purchases **[MY CALC]**).
- **$500/month:** one product only. ~$200 on 2-4 UGC/creator videos + ~$300 on a 7-10 day Meta Advantage+ or TikTok Smart+ test at ~$30-40/day (≈ 1 break-even-CPA ×2-3 per creative cluster) — still below the ~50 events/week threshold on purchase optimisation, so use upstream metrics (CTR ≥1.5-2%, cost per ATC/IC, hook rate) to decide kill/continue; scale only if CPA < break-even CPA with ≥15-20 purchases. Expect most months to net a loss; that is the cost of testing.
- **$1,000/month:** ~$300-400 creative (5-8 videos), ~$600-700 ads over ~2 weeks = ~$45/day. Can run 1 product × 5-6 distinct creatives in a single Advantage+/Smart+ campaign; or 2 products × 1 week. At break-even CPA ≈ $40 this buys ~15-17 purchases: enough for a directional decision, not for statistical confidence. Add retargeting only after 1,000+ site visitors. Consider Google Shopping only if the product is search-driven, and only after compliance setup.
- General: set a hard stop-loss (e.g., stop a product at 2-3× break-even CPA spent with no purchase, per common heuristics — **[UNCERTAIN]**); avoid Q4 (CPM +50-60% Nov vs Jan) for the first tests; don't edit live ad sets (resets learning).

### Inferences
- A beginner with <$500/month cannot run a statistically valid paid test; the economically rational use of $100-500 is creative and organic amplification until a product shows unit economics. A beginner with ~$1,000 should do one disciplined test, not spread across products.
- The dominant risk is the product/margin choice (section 2), not the platform.

### Gaps
- All stage amounts above are my own synthesis; no source specifies a graduation threshold for bootstrapped beginners. The "organic signal" threshold is a judgment.
- Israel-specific: VAT/self-billing impact on budget, USD vs ILS billing, and PayPal FX cost need confirmation with an Israeli accountant.
